*HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM*
    -A web-based system that digitizes the entire clinic workflow

*PROBLEM*- many hospitals especially smaller facilities,rely on manual systems to manage patient information,appointments and treatments.This leads to misdiagnosis, delayed treatment and patient frustration.

*SOLUTION*- This system digitizes the entire clinic workflow :patients book appointments online, doctors access full medical history instantly, prescriptions are recorded digitally, and payments are tracked automatically. No more paper files, no more double bookings, no more lost records.

*TARGET USERS*- Small to medium clinics and hospitals in Kenya with 1–20 doctors who currently manage records manually.

*KEY FEATURES*
-Patient registration and medical history
-Doctor appointment scheduling
-Digital prescriptions
-Treatment records
-Payment tracking
-Login system for staff only


*WHY NOW ?*
Kenya has 10,000+ registered health facilities. Most small clinics cannot afford expensive systems like those used in large hospitals. This system is lightweight, affordable and built for them.
